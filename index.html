The Surplus Principle

Give from the surplus, not the sacrifice

Give Without the Grip — no set amount, no guilt, no lock-in.

No Poor Among Us is built on a central idea: giving should come from your surplus (what's left after your needs and wants are met) — not guilt, not pressure, not a fixed percentage of income. One cannot serve others from an empty tray. Surplus, not sacrifice, is where abundant giving starts.

Why I Started This

A system I could still trust

A while back, I left my church on principle — not because I stopped believing, but because I couldn't keep giving to a system I no longer trusted. Something I've struggled with since is finding a new place for my charitable giving, without enabling people, or giving to organizations that misuse the funds.

For the past year and a half, I've also been organizing support for survivors of abuse through groups like Warrior Women and Round Table, walking alongside mothers fighting to protect their kids, and standing up against corruption. No Poor Among Us was born from this work and a desire to help an increasing number of people in need.

Right now, I help churches and principled organizations raise REAL money for REAL work, personally vetting every organization — ensuring accountability, financial transparency, and outcomes they can show. Long-term, I want to build a place where reputable, vetted organizations, donors, and the people who need help can all meet in one trusted community. This directory is where that journey begins.

If you lead a church or an organization doing hand-up work — not hand-out — and could use help raising support, message me. And if you're simply looking for a place to give with confidence, I'm glad you found us.♥
